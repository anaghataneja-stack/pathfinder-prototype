Behind the scenes

Everything here updates as you answer in the phone. It shows what Pathfinder knows, how it picks each step, and what it stores.

Design rules built in

  • Listings only. Every step links to a live P2E course, Yoma opportunity or imaGen challenge. Listings with no deadline, a broken link or a past deadline are dropped and flagged for review.
  • Every step explains itself. Each one carries a one-line reason. In the live build Azure OpenAI writes it from the listing's own fields; this prototype uses the reviewed templates that under-18s receive.
  • Users can say no. Swap any step, or say none fit and get a lighter or different plan.
  • Buttons, not open chat. Fixed questions with tap or number replies work on basic phones and keep the intake to about 5 minutes.
  • A person after two missed check-ins. Anyone who stalls twice or asks for help goes to a facilitator instead of another automated message.
  • Minimal data. Stores the six answers, the age check, the plan and its status. Reply DELETE to erase them.
All courses, openings, employers and facilitators shown are invented sample data for testing the flow. Prototype date is fixed at Monday 26 October 2026. Estimated running cost in the report: about a third of a US cent per active user each month on GPT‑5.4 nano.
