How Pathfinder uses your data

Pilot draft. GenU's data protection lead must review this notice before real users see it, in line with Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023 and UNICEF's data policy. Contact details marked [ ] are still to be filled in.

What Pathfinder keeps

  • Your answers to the questions (language, age, goal, type of work, state, hours, data budget) and your plan with its progress. These stay on your phone.
  • Skills you tell us about from work you already do, once you confirm them. These also stay on your phone.
  • If you ask for help, or we think you may not be safe, we create a case for a facilitator. A case holds only your state, age group (15–17 or 18–24), language, the plan step you were on and a short case code. It has no name, phone number or message text.
  • If you are 16 or over and say yes, your plan, the skills you confirmed and the steps you finish are added to your Skills Backpack on your Youth Identity (YoID). Nothing goes there unless you agree. You decide when and if you share it.
  • Simple counts (for example "a plan was made", "a step was finished") with your language and age group only. These help GenU see if Pathfinder works. They can't be linked to you.

What Pathfinder does not keep

  • Your name, phone number, email or exact age.
  • Your voice notes. They're turned into text and then thrown away.
  • What you type when you describe your work, after it's been turned into skills.

AI and voice notes

To understand what you type or say, Pathfinder sends that text or voice note to Microsoft's Azure OpenAI service. Before text is sent, phone numbers and emails are removed. A voice note can't be cleaned before it's sent, so please don't say your name or phone number. Azure doesn't use this data to train its AI. You can always skip this step or press the buttons instead.

If you are under 18

A parent or guardian must agree before anything is saved. Your plan reasons come from reviewed messages, not AI writing.

How long it's kept

  • Your plan on your phone: until you reply DELETE, or 6 months without use.
  • Cases: until a facilitator has helped you, then for no more than [ ] months.
  • Counts: for the length of the pilot.

Your rights

You can delete your plan at any time by replying DELETE. To ask what is held about you, or to have a case removed, contact GenU Nigeria at [ ] and quote your case code.
